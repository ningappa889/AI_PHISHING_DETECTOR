CORE CAPABILITIES

Intelligent URL Security Analysis

Multi-layered security engine combining supervised machine learning, natural language processing, and structural heuristics.

🧠

Machine Learning Detection

Supervised Linear SVM classifier analyzes intricate URL patterns learned from large training datasets.

🔤

TF-IDF NLP

Converts URL character n-grams and text substrings into high-dimensional numerical feature vectors.

📐

Structural Analysis

Calculates URL length, domain length, dot counts, subdomain depth, slash frequency, hyphens, and Shannon entropy.

🛡️

Threat Heuristics

Scans for brand spoofing, abused TLD extensions (.top, .online, .cfd), serverless subdomains, and DGA patterns.

📊

Dynamic Risk Score

Computes a continuous 0–100 threat percentage mapped into 5 intuitive color-coded security risk tiers.

💡

Explainable Detection

Provides clear, interpretable bullet-point evidence explaining exactly why a link received its risk score.

RISK CATEGORIZATION

Threat Risk Scale

Five granular security assessment levels mapped by our dynamic risk calculation engine.

0 – 15

Minimal Risk

Verified institutional domains or clean structural links.

16 – 35

Low Risk

Standard clean web domains with no threat markers.

36 – 60

Moderate Risk

Minor structural anomalies or ambiguous lexical signals.

61 – 84

High Risk

Abused TLDs, hyphenated brand lures, or ML threat flags.

85 – 100

Critical Risk

Severe phishing indicators, raw IP hosts, or DGA scams.

ℹ️ Disclaimer: Risk score is an AI/heuristic estimate and does not guarantee that a website is malicious or safe.
